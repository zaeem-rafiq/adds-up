# Screen designs

These 32 pictures are the design of Adds Up: 16 screens, each at desktop and phone width. They were drawn on Friday, November 20, 2026, the day before the hackathon, as planning.

They are pictures only. Every line of the product's code, markup and style was written at the Gemini 4 NYC Hackathon on Saturday, November 21, 2026, after hacking began at 10:30 AM ET.

Four small wording changes were approved after the pictures were drawn. They are listed in `docs/plan.md` and in the unit notes, so a screen can differ from its picture in those words.
